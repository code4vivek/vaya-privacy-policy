# Privacy Policy — Vaya

**Effective date:** October 13, 2026  
**App name:** Vaya Scheduler 
Developer : Vivek Yadav
Contact : connectxvivek@gmail.com

## 1. Overview

Vaya is an offline-first productivity application that helps users organize tasks, goals, roadmaps, habits, reminders, and productivity reports.

Your privacy is important to us. Vaya is designed to provide its core features without requiring an account or transmitting your personal productivity information to a cloud service.

This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, how it is protected, and how you can manage it.

## 2. Information Handled by the App

Depending on the features you use, Vaya may process the following information:

- **Tasks and schedules:** task titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, and completion status.
- **Goals and roadmaps:** goal details, milestones, progress, and related dates.
- **Habits and reports:** habit records, completion history, and statistics generated from your activity.
- **Reminders:** reminder times, scheduling preferences, and information needed to display notifications.
- **App preferences:** theme selection and other settings you configure.

This information is used to provide the app's productivity features.

## 3. Where Your Information Is Stored

Vaya is designed to store your productivity information locally on your device using the app's local database and preferences storage.

The core features do not require account registration or cloud synchronization.

**Vaya does not intentionally upload your locally stored productivity information to a Vaya-operated server as part of its offline-first functionality.**

Information stored on your device may be affected by device backups, operating-system services, or other device-level features enabled by you or your device provider.

## 4. Information Sharing and Sale

Vaya does not sell your personal productivity information.

The app's core functionality is designed without advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics.

We do not intentionally share locally stored task, goal, habit, or productivity-report information with third parties as part of the core offline functionality.

If a future version introduces services that collect, transmit, or share additional information, this policy will be updated to explain those practices before the updated functionality is released.

## 5. Internet and Third-Party Services

Vaya's core productivity functionality is designed to operate without an internet connection.

The app is not designed to require an online account, cloud service, or AI service for its core features.

Android system services, app distribution services, or any third-party components included in a released version may operate under their own applicable privacy practices. Any data handling by components included in the app will be assessed and disclosed where required.

## 6. Notifications

Vaya may schedule local notifications for reminders that you configure.

Notification delivery depends on your device's notification permissions, operating-system settings, battery restrictions, and other system conditions.

You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling notifications may prevent reminders from appearing.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your productivity information is retained locally for as long as it remains stored by the app or its associated device-level storage.

You can remove individual information using available in-app deletion features. Clearing Vaya's app data or uninstalling the app generally removes locally stored app information, subject to Android behavior and any backups or copies outside the app.

If you export information or enable device backups, exported files and backup copies may remain until you delete them using the relevant device or service controls.

Vaya does not currently require an online account for its core functionality. Therefore, account deletion is not applicable to that functionality.

## 8. Data Security

Vaya is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and keep core productivity information on your device.

We rely on Android's application sandbox and the device's security mechanisms to help protect locally stored information. The level of protection also depends on your device security, operating-system version, and settings.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected by a screen lock.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Vaya is a general productivity application. It is not intended to collect personal information from children through an online account or cloud service.

The app's intended audience and any applicable requirements will be considered when publishing and distributing it. If the app's audience or data practices change, this policy will be updated as necessary.

## 10. Your Choices

You can manage your use of Vaya by:

- Editing or deleting information through available app features.
- Changing notification permissions in Android settings.
- Changing app preferences through the settings available in Vaya.
- Managing device backups and exported files through the relevant device or service settings.
- Uninstalling the app if you no longer wish to use it.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published at this URL. Material changes will be reflected in the policy, including an updated effective date where appropriate.
